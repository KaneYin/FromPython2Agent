Projects

Description​

Students will collaborate in groups of 2 to 3 to complete a project on LLM systems. These projects should have some systems and some LLM components. There are two types of projects:

  • Design scalable and accelerated components in MiniTorch for LLMs by reimplementing a recent LLM system paper. or
  • Explore and solve new research challenges on LLM systems with the goal of submitting a paper to a conference (e.g. MLsys, OSDI, SOSP, SC, etc).

For inspiration, browse past student projects, grouped by research topic.

Project Proposal​

Please use the latex style file from here. You proposal should describe:

  • What LLM System problem are you planning to address? -- what are the system challenges?
  • What are the existing state-of-art methods on this problem? Is the source code/model available? 
  • Possible directions for going forward. 
  • How do you evaluate the performance? what kind of workload?
  • Who is your team and how are you planning to split the workload between team members?
  • A rough timeline/milestones
  • What CPU, GPU and storage infrastructure do need for this project? Please estimate the amount of computation time required.  

Project Mid-term Report​

Please include the following sections in your report. There is no page limit but please make your report concise (e.g. max 6 pages)

  • Introduction/Motivation: This essentially lays out the problem definition, motivation, talks about why we need to work on it, the key contributions expected/presented in the work.
  • Related Work/Background: This talks about key papers/works that provide context to your current work. Instead of listing down multiple past works, talk about the ones that minimally differ from your work, and how.
  • Methodology and Implementation Details: This section talks about your method, raises research questions and how you are going to address them.
  • Experiments: This section can describe your experiments and the results you already obtain.
  • Remaining work and timeline.

Project Final Report​

Please include the following sections. You may reuse your mid-term report. There is no page limit but please make your report concise (e.g. max 8 pages)

  • Introduction/Motivation: This essentially lays out the problem definition, motivation, talks about why we need to work on it, the key contributions expected/presented in the work. 
  • Related Work/Background: This talks about key papers/works that provide context to your current work. Instead of listing down multiple past works, talk about the ones that minimally differ from your work, and how. 
  • Methodology: This section talks about your method, raises research questions and how you are going to address them.
  • Implementation Details and Code Snippets.
  • Experiments: This section can describe your experiments and the results you obtain. 
  • Analysis/Ablations/Findings: Typically, you would have multiple factors involved in your experimental setting. Analysis sections help you probe deeper into the results and help piece out contributions from individual modeling decisions made. You should also describe what works and what does not work.
  • Conclusion/Discussion: This would list the main takeaways from your work, discuss some future ideas (if any) and engage in discussion. Please also lay out some known limitations of your work. 
  • Team Member Contributions List out each individual's contributions in this section. Please be as concrete as possible (e.g. Author X contributed to the function in the file ABC.py, Author Y completed experiments for 123.)

Suggested Topics for Projects​

We provide a seed list of project ideas. You are welcome to choose any of the below topics but not restrict to these.
We strongly encourage you to come up with something that is challenging and excites you regarding LLM systems.

Extend scalable components in MiniTorch

  • Implement FlashAttention
  • Implement PageAttention
  • Implement mix-precision training
  • Implement memory optimization in LightSeq for faster inference
  • Implement DPO training (Direct Preference Optimization)

Open Research Ideas

  • Scaling multimodal LLM training and inference (e.g. speech-text LLM model)
    • the input can be long sequence due to a great number of pixels or number of frames in a speech utterance
    • the output can also be long
  • Federated training for large models [ link ]
  • Additional training (e.g., quantized training/finetuning under fp16/int8) for better quantization performance afterwards
  • Special model/system design for biology / chemistry-related models (small vocab_size ~10, but long sequence e.g., 10million)
  • Accelerating large model inference in a browser with limited resource (e.g. how to make WebLLM 10x faster on a 16GB mem laptop?)
  • Scaling and accelerating the training for MOE Models [https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.11217, https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.15841]
  • Training with heterogenous hardware
  • Scalable system to jointly train retrieval embeddings and LLM modules [https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12457]
  • KV Cache Management [https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09665, https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00079, http://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.07104]
