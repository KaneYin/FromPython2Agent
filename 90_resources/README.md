Collections moved as units. Their internal assignment layouts, environments, attribution, and licenses remain with them. The two copies of Agents HW1 remain separate; the coursework copy retains its nested `chess_app` checkout. Course READMEs describe archived material rather than a freshly verified schedule.

Add new course archives under `courses/<institution>/<course_code_and_topic>/`. Keep imported reference collections together, with their source information and setup instructions. Personal implementations belong in the relevant numbered learning stage.

Many PDFs, datasets, weights, and generated files are ignored by Git and exist only in the local archive.
